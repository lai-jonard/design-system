la-guidance

The note under an answer someone has picked: what their answer means and what to do next. Legal writes it for each option of a Review, Decide & Confirm question, and it shows only once that option is picked. Neutral by design: the guidance follows the founder’s own answer, so it must never read as our verdict on it.

Anatomy

We draft board minutes or a directors’ written resolution. Mark the shareholders’ resolution step as done elsewhere: it’s for the other route.
  1. Label. The small heading, “Guidance” by default, in --la-color-text-muted. label="" hides it. part="label".
  2. Body. The default slot: one or two short paragraphs, in --la-color-text on --la-color-bg-subtle, at a 64ch measure.

Usage examples

Under the picked option, in la-question’s guidance slot. Show it only once the option is picked.

<la-question text="Does an investor have a right to approve or appoint directors?"> …the answer… <la-guidance slot="guidance"> <p>Get their consent in writing before you appoint anyone.</p> </la-guidance> </la-question>

Notes

  • When to use. Under a picked option on a playbook step, for legal’s guidance on that option. Show it only once the option is picked, as Polaris’s ChoiceList renders content under a choice only once it’s selected.
  • Announced. la-question’s guidance slot is a polite live region that stays in place, so a screen reader hears the guidance when an option is picked (WCAG 4.1.3 Status Messages). GOV.UK notes that users of assistive technology may not be told when revealed content appears; this closes that gap.
  • Not this. A message about the page or the system is la-alert, and how to fill a field in is the field’s hint.
  • Neutral. A --la-color-bg-subtle box at --la-radius-md, with no status colour, no icon of judgement and no side stripe (decision 93). The guidance follows the founder’s own answer, so it never reads as our verdict on it (decision 117, rule/answers-never-verdicts).
  • Phase B. An answer carries on or asks a follow-up. A stop, or the step that’s for the other route, is said in the guidance itself.
  • Removed 9 Oct 2026. The adds slot and property, which named the steps an answer turns on (“This adds: … It joins your plan when you confirm.”), were cut from Phase B with the other after-launch paths (decision 124; Bobby’s 8 Oct brief, B8). They’re in the north star, and intact at dc95fe47.
  • Consumers. la-step-questions, under the picked option.
