la-thinking

AI is working on something, said in words, with a soft light sweeping across them. For a wait the founder can see coming but doesn’t have to sit through, where there’s nothing concrete to show yet: AI reading a document before it suggests an answer. Built as Vercel AI Elements’ Shimmer is (decision 137). The name was locked for the AI thinking state in loading.md §2.9.

Anatomy

Reading your articles of association…
  1. The words. The default slot: what AI is doing, in the page’s words. Muted colour (--la-color-text-muted).
  2. The band. A soft band of the background colour, transparent at both ends, spread px per character either side of its middle. It sweeps from one side to the other and loops, every duration seconds, linear.
  3. The host. role="status" with aria-live="polite": a screen reader hears the words once when they appear, not the motion.

Content guidance

The words

  • Say what AI is doing, to what. “Reading your articles of association…”, not “Thinking…” or “Loading…”: the founder can tell what it’s for and what will come of it. Name the document as a sentence names it, in lower case (decision 139).
  • One short line. It sits where the result will go; it isn’t the result.
  • No progress it can’t back. No percentage and no time estimate: the wait is indeterminate (loading.md: use a determinate indicator only when the quantity is known).
  • End with an ellipsis (…), the single character, as Vercel’s examples and the AI kits do.

What can go wrong

  • It never ends. Every wait needs its failure: when AI fails, take la-thinking away. In la-step-questions, if the founder saw it reading, one quiet line takes its place: we couldn’t read the document, so answer it as you know it. If they didn’t, the question is simply asked (decision 157).
  • The letters fade where the band passes. At its middle the text nears the background, as Vercel’s does. Keep it short so the line is readable between sweeps; the words are announced once, in full.

Usage examples

On its own.

<la-thinking>Reading your articles of association…</la-thinking>

In la-step-questions, the page sets reading to the document’s own title and the modal draws AI’s panel with the document’s glyph and la-thinking size="sm", naming the document in lower case in its sentence (decision 139). It sets filled when AI is done, or drops the id if AI failed.

modal.reading = { ad_reserve: 'Articles of Association' };

Notes

  • Its keyframes live in its shadow root, as la-spinner’s do. The .t-shimmer utility loading.md named was never built, and a global class wouldn’t reach inside a component.
  • The sweep moves the gradient’s background-position, as Vercel’s does. It’s the one exception to rule/motion-gpu-safe: paint on one short line, only while AI reads, stopped under reduced motion (decision 158).
  • The text is one inline block, so a wrapped line shares one sweep (Vercel’s inline-block).
  • In forced colours the gradient is dropped and the text takes CanvasText.
