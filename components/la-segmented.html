la-segmented

Pick one of two to five short options, in a row: a radio group drawn as one track. la-segmented holds la-segment children, in the same way la-select holds la-option; each segment’s text is its label. An input, not navigation: it can sit in a form beside la-input and la-select, at their heights, and the page reads its value as it reads theirs. la-tabs, even variant="segmented", is navigation: each tab shows a different panel or view. Modelled on Astryx’s SegmentedControl, whose limits and variations it takes.

Anatomy

Grid List Table
  1. Track. The muted rail that holds the segments: --la-color-bg-muted, --la-radius-md. part="track" on la-segmented.
  2. Segment. One option, an la-segment. part="segment".
  3. Icon. Optional, before the label. The icon slot on la-segment.
  4. Label. The segment’s text, its default slot. part="label". With hide-label it is read but not shown.
  5. Picked segment. Raised in --la-color-bg with --la-shadow-xs, in the text colour. Never a status colour.
  6. Tooltip. Only when the control is disabled with a disabled-message: an la-tooltip around the track that says why.

la-segment

One option inside la-segmented. Its text is the label. The parent owns the state: it sets checked, the role and the tab stop, and hears the press. A segment on its own does nothing.

Usage examples

One answer per person on a playbook step, as la-people-answer draws it. Nothing is picked until the founder picks.

<la-segmented size="sm" label="Priya Shah" value=""> <la-segment value="hnw">High-net-worth</la-segment> <la-segment value="ssi">Self-certified sophisticated</la-segment> <la-segment value="unsure">Not sure</la-segment> </la-segmented>

In a form, beside la-input and la-select at the same height. The page reads value, or listens for la-change.

<la-input label="Employee name"></la-input> <la-select label="Contract">…</la-select> <la-segmented label="Hours" value="full"> <la-segment value="full">Full time</la-segment> <la-segment value="part">Part time</la-segment> </la-segmented>

A view switch with icons. A view switch sets value; an answer never does.

<la-segmented label="View" value="list"> <la-segment value="grid" hide-label><i slot="icon" class="ph ph-squares-four"></i>Grid</la-segment> <la-segment value="list" hide-label><i slot="icon" class="ph ph-list"></i>List</la-segment> </la-segmented>

Notes

  • When to use. Two to five short options, picked inline, where every option should stay in sight: the answer per person on a step, or a switch between views or modes on a workbench or toolbar.
  • Not this. Options that need a description or a Recommended mark are la-choice in a radiogroup. Six or more options, or long labels, are la-select. Moving between pages or panels is la-tabs, and a single on or off is la-switch. Don’t wrap a disabled control in la-tooltip to say why: it swallows the hover, so use disabled-message.
  • Neutral. The picked segment is raised, never primary or a status colour, because on a playbook step the pick is the founder’s answer, not our verdict (decision 117). Nothing is picked until someone picks.
  • Where it differs from Astryx, on purpose. Astryx always has one option picked; here nothing is picked until someone picks, and a view switch sets value. Astryx truncates a long label; here it wraps, because the founder has to read the whole answer.
  • Contrast, deferred (Jonard, 8 October 2026). Labels meet 4.5:1 in every state, an icon with no label 3:1, and the focus ring 3:1. The picked segment is told apart by its raised white (1.09:1 on the track) and a darker label (2.03:1 against the unpicked one), below the 3:1 Astryx asks for where people rely on the selected look. A 3:1 edge on the picked segment is the likely fix; it isn’t added for now.
  • Keyboard and ARIA. The host is role="radiogroup" named by label, and each segment is role="radio" with aria-checked. One tab stop: the picked segment, or the first enabled one. Arrow keys move and pick (Left and Up go back, Right and Down go on, wrapping round), Home and End go to the ends, and Space or Enter picks the focused segment. Disabled segments are skipped and carry aria-disabled. With disabled-message the control keeps a tab stop and is described by the message. Under forced colours the picked segment gets an outline.
  • Targets and motion. Each segment pads to 44px on a touch screen. Background, colour and shadow change on --dur-fast --ease, and are instant under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Consumers. la-people-answer (one answer per person, sm).
