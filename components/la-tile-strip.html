la-tile-strip + la-tile

A pressable tile group. la-tile is one tile: a 36px glyph disc, a label, a quiet count line, a pressed state. la-tile-strip lays tiles out in a frameless grid with hairlines between them, collapses 4 → 2 → 1 columns with its container (2 under 900px, 1 under 440px), and in single-selection mode presses one tile and lifts the others.

Anatomy

Square at rest, rounded the moment there is a surface to round (hover, pressed). The pressed tile is the darker surface and nothing louder.

la-tile-strip

A frameless, pressable tile group with counts — the legal areas as the filter over the list they narrow. Hairlines between the tiles, never a card around them.

Usage

  • Sub-labels are counts in the words of the current scope: "3 documents", "2 to add", "1 for review". Paint them off the data; the authored text is only the first paint.
  • Single selection (default): set value on the strip to move the press; listen for la-tile-change. The empty value is a legitimate tile ("All Areas").
  • Slot a rendered object as slot="icon" (24px) when the area has one; the disc's tint comes off so the object carries its own colour.
  • No count badges on tiles — one queue, one count, on the control that opens it (LawVault, 11 Aug 2026).

Notes

Frameless by decision (Jonard, 17 Sep 2026): the strip is a filter, not a card that acts, so it takes no surface of its own (rule/one-card-surface). Replaces .area-strip / .area-tile on LawVault; the Dashboard's area tiles are the second consumer. Decision 99.
