la-question

One question on a playbook step: legal’s question, a quiet What this means where legal wrote a gloss, the answer, the guidance under the option picked, and any follow-up the answer asks. The question is its answer’s name, and the screen’s heading when it’s alone on the screen (decision 149). It doesn’t know its answer: the page owns that and fills the slots. In the step, la-step-questions asks one at a time in a modal.

Anatomy

  1. Question. Legal’s words, at the large text size. They’re the answer’s name, or its group’s, and the screen’s heading when heading-level is set (decision 149, Q1).
  2. What this means. la-disclosure’s quiet row, under the question, when legal wrote a gloss.
  3. Error. What’s missing, once the page’s button is pressed too soon: the forms foundation’s error, led by the warning glyph, under a field and above a list of choices (decision 140, amended 15 Oct 2026). It goes as soon as the answer is given.
  4. Answer. The default slot: the page’s choices, field or upload.
  5. Guidance. The guidance slot, under the answer once an option is picked: la-guidance, in a polite live region that grows in.
  6. Follow-up. The follow slot: questions the answer asks, indented under a hairline.

The question is its answer’s name, and the screen’s heading when it’s alone on the screen (decision 149). It doesn’t know its answer: the page owns that and fills the slots. In the step, la-step-questions asks one at a time in a modal.

Properties

PropertyAttributeTypeDefaultDescription
text text string '' the question, legal's words; also its answer's name
means means string '' the plain-English gloss behind *What this means* (a quiet la-disclosure); none hides it
headingLevel heading-level number 0 attribute heading-level: the question is the screen's heading at this level (2–6), for a screen that asks one question; 0 (default), it isn't a heading
error error string '' what's missing, when the page's button was pressed too soon: under a field, above a list of choices (decision 140, amended 15 Oct 2026)

Slots

SlotDescription
(default) the answer: inputs, choices, a record row. The first element is the one the question names
guidance la-guidance for the picked option. Its wrapper is a polite live region that stays in place, so a screen reader hears the guidance when an option is picked (WCAG 4.1.3; the gap GOV.UK notes for revealed content)
follow follow-up la-question elements, one level down

Methods

MethodWhat it does
focusText()Moves focus to the question’s words, for a page that has just shown a new question in place of the last.
focusAnswer()Moves focus to the answer: the group’s tab stop, or into the field’s own control. For a press with the answer missing (decision 140).

No events of its own: its answer’s controls fire theirs, and the page keeps the answer.

Usage examples

A yes or no question with legal’s gloss, alone on the screen; the page fills the answer, and the question names it.

<la-question text="Does an investor have a right to approve or appoint directors?" means="Usually set in a shareholders’ agreement after a funding round." heading-level="3"> <div class="opts yn" role="radiogroup">…two la-choice…</div> </la-question>

Pressed too soon: say what’s missing, and move focus to the answer.

question.error = 'Select an answer.'; await question.updateComplete; question.focusAnswer();

Guidance once an option is picked.

<la-guidance slot="guidance"><p>Get their consent in writing before you appoint anyone.</p></la-guidance>

Notes

  • Where it’s used. Inside la-step-questions, one question to a screen, as the screen’s heading. Laid flat, as on the step’s states page, questions stack with space between them.
  • Why the words are set as the answer’s name. aria-labelledby can’t reach from a field’s shadow root to the question’s, and a legend in the page’s own DOM wouldn’t reach la-input’s input either. Each field already hands its aria-label to its own control, so a screen reader hears the heading, then the same words on the field (decision 149, Q1C).
  • It holds no answer and runs no rule. The page decides what’s picked, what guidance shows and which follow-ups appear (decisions 116 and 117). Every question is required (decision 127).
  • Follow-ups. In the modal a follow-up is its own screen; the follow slot is for a question laid flat.
  • Guidance appears only once an option is picked, and says what that answer means for the founder (la-guidance, decision 117).
  • Copy is legal’s, from the CMS (07): the question, the gloss and the guidance.
